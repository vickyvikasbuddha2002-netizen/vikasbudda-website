A day with Roam

Always with you, never just sitting there.

Roam does not wait to be poked. It notices you, checks on you and keeps finding small ways to say hello.

  1. 9:00 am

    You sit down

    It spots your face, says hello by name and rolls over to meet you.

  2. 11:30 am

    You are deep in work

    Its eyes follow you. Now and then it hums to itself, or peeks over to see what you are doing.

  3. 1:00 pm

    Lunch time

    It asks if you have eaten, and keeps asking until you answer.

  4. 2:00 pm

    You step out for a meeting

    Guard mode. It faces your chair, stares down anyone who comes close and tells you who stopped by when you are back.

  5. 4:00 pm

    You laugh, or you sigh

    A laugh and it laughs along. A long sigh and it comes closer with a worried face.

  6. 6:30 pm

    You leave

    It sums up your day, says a small goodbye and dozes off until you are back.

Made for the office

A desk mate with real jobs.

Most companion robots only play. Roam also helps you get through a working day, and every one of these is a button you can try above.

  1. Desk guard

    While you are in a meeting

    Roam faces your chair and keeps watch. When you are back it tells you who stopped by. It keeps only counts and times, never photos.

  2. Focus shield

    When you need deep work

    Hold its head and it turns to face the aisle with a do not disturb face. Visitors are asked, politely, to come back later.

  3. Message keeper

    When someone comes by

    A colleague can leave a short voice message with Roam. It plays it back the moment you sit down again.

  4. Hydration Puck link

    Works with my other project

    Roam talks to the Hydration Puck under your bottle. If the bottle has not moved in an hour, Roam rolls over and reminds you to drink.

  5. End of day recap

    When you pack up

    Focus time, visitors, messages and water, in four short lines. Then it says goodnight.

  6. Comes when you knock

    In a quiet office

    Knock twice anywhere on your desk and Roam turns toward the sound and rolls over. No talking needed.

  7. Posture mirror

    While you work

    Roam copies how you sit. Slouch and it droops with you; sit up and it perks up.

  8. Keeps the office calm

    On loud calls

    When your voice gets loud, Roam shows a small, polite reminder to bring it down.

  9. Rubber-duck mode

    When you are stuck

    Explain the problem to Roam. It listens, nods and asks why, and saying it out loud often solves it.

Accessories

Snap one on, and Roam gets a new job.

Each accessory clicks onto the pad on top of Roam. Roam recognises which one it is wearing and changes what it does, and how it behaves.

  1. Plant pod

    Roam becomes a plant-sitter

    A tiny succulent rides on Roam's head. It finds the brightest spot on your desk, parks there, and reminds you to water it.

  2. Phone dock

    Notifications with feelings

    Your phone rests on Roam's sloped back. Important calls get its full attention; spam gets a bored look.

  3. Desk flag

    For shared offices

    While you are away, Roam parks in the middle of your desk and shows when you will be back.

  4. Note tray

    Messages on paper

    A shallow tray for a pen and sticky notes. Colleagues leave a note, and Roam tells you when you sit down.

  5. Wheel discs

    Personal style

    The large discs on Roam's sides come off. Print your own colours and patterns, like changing a watch strap.

Designed like a product

Every detail on the outside is deliberate.

Roam is modelled to look like something you could buy, not a project held together with tape. Drag it around above and look closely.

  1. Faceted, not rounded

    Every edge is a crisp 45° chamfer. The head reads as an octagon from the front, the side and the top, and the body tapers up towards the neck.

  2. A face behind dark glass

    The screen sits under an octagonal black glass panel with a thin terracotta bezel. When Roam sleeps, the face disappears into one dark panel.

  3. Terracotta and charcoal

    A matte terracotta shell over charcoal parts: the neck, the base and the wheels. Its eyes glow from ice blue to lilac.

  4. A button with a light line

    One square button on the side of its head, with a thin light line, wakes Roam up. Small slots on the other side let it hear you.

  5. Big wheels, low base

    Two large wheels sit inside arched pockets in its sides, so the body frames them. A black base underneath hides the ball caster and every screw.

  6. Sound and power at the back

    Vents on the back of the head let its speaker breathe, and the USB-C port sits low on the back of the body.
