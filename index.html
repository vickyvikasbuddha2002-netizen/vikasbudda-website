    Project 02

    Hydration Puck

    A smart hydration tracker, designed in Siemens NX, wired and built by hand.

    The problem

    People forget to drink water.

    Apps and wearables ask for too much. Most people already keep a bottle on the desk.

    The idea

    Turn any bottle into a smart one.

    Put the bottle on the puck. That is the whole setup.

    How it works

    Lift, sip, set it down.

    The puck weighs the bottle before and after. Lighter means a sip. Heavier means a refill, so nothing is counted.

    The build

    Designed in Siemens NX. Printed on a Bambu Lab A1. Wired by hand.

    Every enclosure part and internal mount was modelled for fit and printing, then assembled and tested.

    The result

    Every sip, logged.

    Daily intake, sips and achievements on its own screen, plus a phone dashboard over its own Wi-Fi.

    Siemens NXEnclosure designed for fit and DFM
