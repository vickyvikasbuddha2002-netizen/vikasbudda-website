01 Overview

A calmer, smarter workspace.

Omvi notices you, moves with you and responds. A companion for long workdays, not another screen.

02 Abilities

Sees and understands.

It looks up when you sit down and follows you around the desk with its eyes.

Move your cursor. Omvi is watching.

Tap Omvi to say hello.

02 Abilities

Moves with you.

No wake word in a quiet office. Knock twice on the desk, and Omvi rolls over.

Or double-tap anywhere on this screen.

02 Abilities

Talks and listens.

Omvi answers in its own language, with English underneath.

Scripted demo. The real Omvi will reply through an AI model.

02 Abilities

Helps you focus.

It notices how you sit, how loud the room gets and how long you have been at it.

02 Abilities

Keeps watch.

Step away, and Omvi guards your desk. It tells you who stopped by. Counts and times, never photos.

02 Abilities

A face that feels.

A full-colour face, so every mood has its own light.

03 Design

Minimal form. Maximum presence.

One soft sloped body, one glass face, wheels tucked into the sides. Calm on a desk, expressive when it needs to be.

04 Engineering

Built with purpose.

Designed in Siemens NX around real parts. Five shell parts, no visible screws.

  1. Top touch pad
  2. Face glass and display
  3. Main shell
  4. Drive wheels
  5. Base
